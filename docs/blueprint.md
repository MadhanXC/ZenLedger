# **App Name**: QuikQuote Pro

## Core Features:

- Company Profile Management: Allows users to input and manage their company details, including name, address, contact information, logo, bank details, and GST number, which are essential for professional documentation.
- Client Management System: Enable creation, editing, and viewing of client profiles, including contact information and a historical overview of quotes and invoices.
- Product/Service Catalog: Maintain a catalog of standard products or services, each with predefined names, descriptions, unit prices, and configurable GST rates, for efficient document creation.
- Quote & Estimate Creator: Provide an intuitive interface for generating new quotes and estimates. Users can add line items from the catalog, apply custom rates, discounts, and automatic GST calculations.
- Invoice Generation & Tracking: Functionality to convert approved quotes into invoices or create standalone invoices. Includes tools for tracking payment status, recording payments, and marking invoices as paid.
- Description Tool: Assist users in crafting clear, concise, and professional product or service descriptions for quotes and invoices, based on keywords or short inputs.
- PDF Export & Print: Generate high-quality PDF documents for quotes and invoices, ensuring a professional appearance for printing and sharing with clients.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A sophisticated charcoal gray (#343A40) for a strong, reliable, and professional aesthetic, serving as the main brand color.
- Background color: A clean, crisp off-white (#F8F9FA) to provide a spacious and highly readable canvas for all content.
- Accent color: A refined deep teal (#20C997) to highlight interactive elements, calls to action, and important information, adding a touch of modernity and freshness.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) will be used for all textual content, offering excellent legibility and a contemporary, objective aesthetic suitable for business documentation.
- Utilize a set of clean, line-based icons that clearly represent their functions without clutter, maintaining a professional and streamlined appearance.
- Adopt a clean, spacious layout with clear visual hierarchies. Information should be organized logically, mirroring the structure of professional business documents for ease of navigation and comprehension.
- Incorporate subtle, functional animations for feedback on user actions (e.g., successful saving, item added to quote) to enhance the user experience without being distracting.
